# Ecosystem logo sources

Most marks come from official organization sites or official institutional project pages. The undated ISMAR mark is sourced from a conference listing and cross-checked against ISMAR's official symbol. White raster backgrounds were removed from CAMP and ISMAR without changing their marks. The existing TUM, MRI, and DFG files in `/public/images/logos/` are reused from this repository.
